02 Galápagos · travel photo
Galápagos Islands

A place that felt completely different from everyday life

Traveling to the Galápagos was one of the experiences that reminded me how much I enjoy stepping outside familiar settings. Being in a place shaped so visibly by nature made the world feel larger, slower, and more surprising.

Experiences like this are meaningful to me because they interrupt routine. They give me space to notice more carefully, stay curious, and return home with a slightly wider perspective.

I like experiences that make the familiar feel a little less fixed.

SUNY Oneonta · New York · 2019

My semester as an exchange student

In 2019, I spent a semester at SUNY Oneonta in New York as an exchange student. Living and studying in a different cultural environment gave me the chance to step outside routines that had always felt familiar.

The experience made me more attentive to context: how differently people communicate, build relationships, and understand the same situation depending on where they come from. It also taught me to become comfortable with being new, asking questions, and finding my way in an unfamiliar place.

New environments have often been where I notice the most about people—and about myself.

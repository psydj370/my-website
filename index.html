About

I am a researcher interested in clinical psychology, digital mental health, and intensive longitudinal methods. My work focuses on understanding how psychological risk changes over time and how technology can be used to deliver support at moments when it is most needed.

Research Interests

Suicide Prevention

Understanding short-term changes in psychological risk and identifying periods of heightened vulnerability.

Digital Mental Health

Using digital technologies to improve access to timely and personalized psychological support.

Intensive Longitudinal Methods

Using ecological momentary assessment, daily diary, and within-person approaches to study psychological processes.

Adaptive Interventions

Developing interventions that respond dynamically to changes in psychological risk over time.

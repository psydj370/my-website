Welcome to my Website

I am a clinician working in community addiction and mental health care in South Korea. I earned my MA and BA in Psychology from Chungnam National University, with a minor in Computer Science during my undergraduate studies, where my research examined how psychological, genetic, and environmental factors interact over time to shape behavior. My research experience has included longitudinal research, intervention studies, meta-analysis, and intensive daily data.

My long-term research focus is suicide prevention. I am especially interested in understanding when suicide risk rises within an individual, what support is most likely to help, and how that support can reach people in time. I hope to pursue doctoral training to further develop this work by integrating machine-learning approaches, digital mental health tools, and individualized intervention research to build more precise and timely approaches to suicide prevention across clinical and public mental health settings.

Working to make hope more viral than despair :)

Long-Term Direction

From prediction to prevention systems

Individual level

Understand risk

Build better models of when suicide risk rises within a specific person using repeated and real-world data rather than one-time snapshots.

Intervention level

Personalize support

Use those insights to guide support that better matches the person's needs, context, and available resources.

System level

Strengthen prevention systems

Translate research into tools and strategies that can work in hospitals, community organizations, and public mental health systems in Korea and beyond.

My long-term goal is not simply to build a better risk model. It is to help create suicide-prevention systems that are more precise, more responsive, and more capable of reaching people before a moment of crisis becomes irreversible.

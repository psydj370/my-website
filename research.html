Research Projects

Daily Dynamics, Genetic Susceptibility, and Drinking

14-day daily diary · N = 120 · 84.5% compliance · Multilevel modeling

This project examined drinking as a process that unfolds within individuals across days. Using intensive longitudinal data, the study tested how daily psychological experiences and a cumulative genetic susceptibility score were associated with next-day drinking.

Why it matters: Daily data can reveal patterns that are difficult to observe when behavior is measured only once or averaged across long periods.

Chat-Based Motivational Interviewing

Digital intervention · 3 × 50-minute sessions · MITI 4

This project examined how motivational interviewing principles could be delivered through technology-mediated communication and evaluated intervention fidelity using the Motivational Interviewing Treatment Integrity framework.

Why it matters: Digital delivery may expand where and when psychological interventions can occur while preserving meaningful therapeutic processes.

Gaming, Aggression, and Parenting Across Development

Five-year longitudinal study · Reciprocal associations

This research examined longitudinal relationships among gaming, aggression, and parenting over time, with attention to how individual behavior and interpersonal environments can influence one another.

Why it matters: Longitudinal designs help distinguish developmental pathways that cannot be captured through cross-sectional snapshots.

Comparative Effects of Psychological Interventions

Network meta-analysis · 60 randomized controlled trials

This project synthesized evidence across randomized controlled trials to compare multiple psychological intervention approaches within a single analytic framework.

Why it matters: Comparative evidence can help identify which intervention approaches are most strongly supported across existing studies.
